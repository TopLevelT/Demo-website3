We use advanced acoustic and thermal technology to pinpoint hidden leaks behind walls and under slabs. We find it first, so you don't have to tear up your floor.

High Water Bill? You might have a "Ghost Leak."

A pinhole leak in a copper pipe behind your drywall can waste 3,000 gallons of water a year before you ever see a wet spot. By the time you see mold or peeling paint, the damage is already done.

Don't let a plumber guess. Many unqualified techs will start cutting holes in your wall hoping to find the damp spot. At ProFix Local, we use science, not guesswork.

Our Non-Invasive Tech

🌡️

Thermal Imaging

Our FLIR cameras detect temperature differences of 0.1 degrees, revealing cool spots behind walls where water is evaporating.

🎧

Ultrasonic Sonar

We use sensitive ground microphones to "hear" the hiss of a leaking pipe, even through 6 inches of concrete slab.

The Detection Process

1. Meter Test

We shut off the main valve and check the meter to confirm the leak rate and verify it's not a toilet running.

2. Isolation

We isolate the system (Hot vs Cold, Yard vs House) to narrow the search area.

3. Pinpoint & Repair

We mark the exact spot with tape. You then choose: we fix it now, or you hire someone else. No obligation.
