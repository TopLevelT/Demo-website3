Repair or Replace?

The average water heater lasts 8-12 years. If yours is leaking, making rumbling noises, or failing to provide enough hot water, you have a decision to make.

Our technicians carry the most common parts for Bradford White, Rheem, and AO Smith units, meaning we can fix 90% of issues on the first visit. However, if your unit is over 10 years old, replacement is often the more cost-effective choice.

Water Heater FAQs

How do I know if I need a new heater? +

If your unit is over 10 years old, leaking, or producing rusty water, it is time to replace. Repairing an old unit is often throwing good money after bad.

How long does installation take? +

A standard swap takes 2-4 hours. A tankless conversion typically takes 1 full day due to gas line and venting upgrades.
